Pizza Recipe

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish consisting of a flat round base of dough baked with a topping of tomato sauce and cheese, typically with added meat or vegetables. It's a versatile and delicious meal that can be customized to suit any taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your choice of toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, etc.)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top.
  6. Add your desired toppings.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
