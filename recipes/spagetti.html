Spagetti Recipe

Description

Spagetti is a classic Italian pasta dish made with long, thin noodles and a variety of sauces. It's a simple yet delicious meal that can be enjoyed by people of all ages.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spagetti noodles
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook spagetti noodles according to package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat.
  3. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the skillet and simmer for 5-10 minutes.
  5. Drain the cooked spagetti noodles and add them to the skillet with the sauce.
  6. Toss the noodles in the sauce until well coated.
  7. Serve hot, topped with grated Parmesan cheese and a sprinkle of salt and pepper.
