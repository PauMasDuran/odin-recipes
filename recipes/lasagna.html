Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It's a hearty and comforting meal that's perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Brown ground beef in a large skillet.
  4. Add marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Mix ricotta cheese with egg and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown.
